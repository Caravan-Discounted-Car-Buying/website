Privacy note for the Caravan waitlist

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Caravan is an early-stage project. This page covers only the waitlist form on this website. There is no account, no payment and no real dealer involved here.

What we collect

  • Your email address (required).
  • Whether you're a buyer or a dealer (required).
  • Your city and a car make you're interested in, only if you type them in.
  • The date and time you joined.

That's all the form stores. This site uses no analytics, no trackers, no advertising scripts and no cookies. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third party.

Why we collect it

  • To email you updates about Caravan, such as when there's a demo worth trying.
  • If you joined as a dealer, to contact you about how group bidding might work for you.

We don't sell your details, and we don't share them with dealers, manufacturers or advertisers.

Waitlist entries are saved to a private Google Sheet owned by the project team, using a Google Apps Script. That means Google stores this data on our behalf, under Google's own terms. Only the Caravan team can open the sheet.
